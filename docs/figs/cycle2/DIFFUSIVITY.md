## The formula

```
D = k / (eta * phi * beta)
```

`phi` and `beta` enter the governing equations only as their product (`setup_model.m:101` says so explicitly), so neither is separately identifiable from a pressure record.

Near the well the two models agree to 30%. The far field differs by a factor of 128, and the reason is the CONTRAST: Taiyi's fault zone is a 2.75x step, ours is 250x. That is the structural difference between the models -- not the viscosity, and not the near-well value.

### The caveat that belongs with this

A diffusivity fitted to a triggering front is convention-dependent: the same envelope under r = sqrt(4Dt) rather than sqrt(4 pi D t) gives a D that is pi times larger. Shapiro (2000) calls the step that produces the 4 pi "of partially heuristic character". The back-front 4D is derived rather than conventional, which is why 0.154 is used as the reference above. Even with a factor of pi either way, the ordering here does not change.
